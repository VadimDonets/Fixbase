Network - Laptops & Phones

Wi-Fi not connecting

Your device cannot connect to Wi-Fi, or the connection keeps dropping.

Beginner-friendly Safe first checks
OVERVIEW

What might be happening?

Wi-Fi issues can come from temporary connection glitches, router problems or outdated network settings. Start with simple checks before changing anything advanced.

STEP-BY-STEP

Things to try

  1. 01

    Check Wi-Fi settings

    Make sure Wi-Fi is switched on and Airplane Mode is off. Try reconnecting to your usual network.

  2. 02

    Restart the connection

    Turn Wi-Fi off, wait a few seconds, then turn it back on. Restart your device if needed.

  3. 03

    Check another device

    See whether another device can use the same Wi-Fi network. If not, your router or internet connection might be the issue.

  4. 04

    Use the built-in troubleshooter

    Open your operating system’s network settings and try its built-in diagnostics.

Safety first

If the connection still fails across several devices, contact your internet provider or a technician.

NEED MORE HELP?

Still having trouble?

If basic checks don't help, contact the device manufacturer or a qualified repair professional.
